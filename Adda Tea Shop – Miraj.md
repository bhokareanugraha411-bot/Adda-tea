## An adda is a place to meet

In Marathi, an adda is where friends gather without a plan. Ours is a small tea shop in Bramhanpuri, Miraj, where the kettle is always on and the chai is made fresh for every cup.

Students from the nearby school, neighbours on their evening walk, and people passing by all end up at the same corner. One cup turns into a long conversation.

## How a cup is made

1. **Boil the water**Fresh water, rolling boil.
2. **Add tea leaves**Strong leaves go in and the colour starts to deepen.
3. **Crush the ginger and spices**Ginger and cardamom, pounded fresh.
4. **Pour in the milk**It rises, we lower the flame, and it rises again.
5. **Strain and serve**Poured hot into your cup, straight away.
